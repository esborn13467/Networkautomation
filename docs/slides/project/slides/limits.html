Limitations

What these results do not show

Simulated traffic

Scripted users are more regular than real ones; real traffic would bring more false alarms.

Simulated night-time

Off-hours activity was marked, not observed at night.

Small lab

One access switch, three users, one laptop with 8 GB of RAM.

Known attack types

New kinds of attack, such as lateral movement over SSH, were not tested.
